# 0006 A contradiction changes its line and asks about dependents

Context: a changed claim often underlies the overview message, other sections' logic and emphasis notes. Editing them silently rewrites agreed logic the author did not touch; leaving them makes the record inconsistent.

Decision: the direct line is changed at once with a note of what it replaced; every dependent agreed part is listed with a question and a recommended disposition; none is edited on the skill's judgment; affected drafts are marked stale.

Alternatives: confirm before applying anything (the author answers a question to make the statement they just made); apply everything as judged and report (the silent cascade the record exists to prevent).

Consequences: a contradiction costs the author one question per dependent; the record may hold a changed claim and an unchanged dependent for a turn, visibly, until the author answers.
