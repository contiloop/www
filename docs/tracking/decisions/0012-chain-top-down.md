# 0012 The chain is built top down, with named relations at every level

Context: in two live sessions the record reached section logic with an empty Progression and section plans that were topic lists; asked where the whole document's chain was, the skill itself named the rules of `references/design-format.md` it had not followed (a progression one step per line, a plan that says how the section unfolds). The relations between claims were settled only while part 3 was being patched sentence by sentence, after a draft already existed, so each one cost a correction instead of a question, and the section joins were never settled at all. The rules were written; the order that would have made the skill follow them was not.

Decision: descent. A section's unfolding is proposed only when the Progression exists; a section's detailed logic only when that section's unfolding exists; a draft only from the detailed logic. When the author's request lands below what exists, the missing upper piece is proposed first, in two or three alternatives, and the accepted one recorded before the skill continues down. The Progression and every section plan are steps joined by named relations from the part 3 set, in one layout (`→ by <relation>` at the head of each later step), so part 3 inherits the relation at each step boundary instead of inventing it. Before any proposal or question at `structure`, `plan` or `logic`, the reply carries three lines: what the level obliges, what the record settles, what is asked or proposed now. A record check compares the Progression and a part 2 entry with that shape whenever a line enters them and at the start of every turn, states the result in one line, changes no line, and lists a fault as an open question that holds descent. A join the author declines is written `→ (declined)`, drafted with no connective, and blocks nothing. What the author states at any level is recorded as stated.

Alternatives: keep the free order and rely on the rules as written (two sessions showed the rules read and not applied; the missing order, not the missing rule, was the cause); block on a declined relation until the author names one (a declined question is the author's decision, and the record already holds declined questions without blocking); a check that judges whether a relation is the right one (that judgment is the author's, and a check that makes it would move the record on the skill's reading).

Consequences: the author may still start anywhere, and what they state is recorded where it lands; the skill's first proposal is always the missing upper chain, so a draft request on an empty record ends in a Progression proposal; replies at `structure`, `plan` and `logic` are three lines longer; an existing record whose section plans are topic lists has those sections drop to `none` at the next turn, with a notice, and the fixture record gains a Progression with named relations and an `Unfolds:` block for the Introduction so the acceptance run still ends in a draft; the independent check's inputs, the type rules files and the 34 sentence rules are unchanged.
