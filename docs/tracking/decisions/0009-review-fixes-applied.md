# 0009 Independent-check findings the record settles are applied and shown; the rest are asked

Context: in the live session of 2026-11-07 the independent check returned findings whose fix the author had already settled by accepting the record (a dropped condition, a weakened role, a sentence with no claim behind it), and under decision 0004 each still cost the author a decision before the draft moved. One finding was shown as the literal fix, a fragment the author could not read as text. The author approved what the record had already decided and read what was not a sentence.

Decision: every finding is still shown, and falls into one of two categories. Category A, the record stands and the draft moves toward it: applied to `draft.md` before the text is shown, the self-check rerun, no second independent check, never applied to text the author typed. Category B, the fix would move the record (a claim, condition, relation, premise or contrast added, removed or changed; a claimless sentence whose removal breaks the chain; a finding that misreads the record; a fix needing a sentence the record has no line for): shown with its three fields, the record line and the proposed change, and asked; the record changes first, then the draft; a sentence under a pending category B finding gets no category A fix meanwhile. Each applied fix is shown as the complete sentence before and after in the draft's language with a one-line reason, the whole paragraph when its flow changed, then the whole section, and the author is asked whether any fix is objected to. With it comes the reading check, comparison 8 of the self-check: the section read in sentence order, each sentence judged against the claims rendered so far, so that an undefined term, two sentences that cannot both render the record, a connective that promises what the record does not draw, or a chain rendered as a list is caught before the text is shown.

Alternatives: finding-by-finding approval, decision 0004 (the author decides what the record already decided, and reads findings instead of text); showing the fixed text and writing it only after the author's word (the same approval cost on every full-section draft, and the draft on disk disagrees with the draft shown); a second independent check after the fixes (a second subagent run per section for drift the self-check already tests, and a loop with no end condition).

Consequences: the author reads sentences before and after rather than findings; a rejected fix restores the prior sentence unless the author gives wording, and its finding becomes category B; in the whole-document tempo only a category B finding holds the next section; the independent check's inputs, dispatch and return are unchanged and stay visible in the transcript; decision 0004 is superseded.
