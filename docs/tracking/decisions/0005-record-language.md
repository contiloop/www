# 0005 The record is English with the author's words verbatim beside each line

Context: the recorded distortion ("must be developed" → "offers an alternative") happened at the Korean-to-English step. The author's rule for documents an AI reads is English. A record holding only the AI's English paraphrase would move the distortion into the authority every check compares against.

Decision: every line in parts 1 to 4 is an English line; when the author's statement was not that English line, the author's words are kept verbatim beside it, in their language; every written line is quoted back in the reply so the author can object at once.

Alternatives: English only, quoted back (the author sees the paraphrase but the record loses what was said); the document's language (the same as English for the author's papers); Korean (the AI reads it less precisely, and the draft is English).

Consequences: the record is longer; a reader can see where each English line departed from the author's statement; restatements are checked against the English line, not the draft.
