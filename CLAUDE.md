# www: I Know How to Write

A plugin for one author, for Claude Code (`/www:write`) and Codex (`$write`), with one skill, `write`: it settles a document's type, reader, message, structure, claim-by-claim logic and wording with the author through questions and proposals, records what is agreed in `design.md` in the document's folder, and drafts `draft.md` so the text says exactly what was agreed. Markdown instructions and three shell scripts; no server, no database, no network. The skill text exists once, in `src/skills/write/`; the two installable packages are generated from it.

## Hard gates

- Nothing enters parts 1 to 4 of a `design.md` without the author's acceptance, and every written line is quoted back in the reply that wrote it with the author's words verbatim beside the English line.
- Text the author typed, in `design.md` or `draft.md`, is never overwritten; a departure from the record is a question, not a fix. Dependents of a changed line are asked about, never edited silently.
- Every sentence drafted answers to a recorded claim, relation or accepted addition, with its role, strength and conditions kept; findings of the independent check change the draft only on the author's word.
- The skill writes only `design.md`, `draft.md` and `~/.claude/www/writing-rules.md`; nothing into its own installed files.
- Skill text is edited only in `src/skills/write/` and names no platform; `claude/` and `codex/plugin/` are regenerated by `scripts/build.sh`, never edited; `scripts/check.sh` exits 0 before any commit touching `src/`, `platform/`, `claude/` or `codex/`. Skill files are English, dense, general rules with no question lists or document-type templates. The full rules are in `docs/standards.md`.

## Before working

Read `docs/standards.md`, `docs/engineering-notes.md` and `src/skills/write/AGENTS.md`. Then, by kind of work:

- Changing how the record is written or read: `src/skills/write/references/design-format.md` first; the Status value sets and the claim notation are a contract that `tests/ads-introduction/design.md` and `docs/contracts.md` depend on.
- Changing drafting or checks: `references/drafting.md` and `references/review.md`, then rerun the acceptance fixture per `tests/ads-introduction/RUN.md`; `writing-rules.md` rule numbers 26, 27 and 30 are cited elsewhere, so do not renumber.
- Changing questioning: `references/elicit.md`; the fixture does not exercise it, so try the change live on a real document.
- Adding a style source (the planned reference-sentence collection): the list in `references/drafting.md`, nowhere else.
- After any edit under `src/` or `platform/`: raise the three manifest versions, run `sh scripts/build.sh && sh scripts/check.sh`, commit the regenerated packages, then update the installed copies as `docs/operations.md` describes before testing.

## When something goes wrong

Report to the author at once: the skill wrote into a manuscript, overwrote text the author typed, wrote into its own installed files, or put a line into parts 1 to 4 that the author did not accept; or the two generated packages carry different skill text. These break the author's authority over the document and are never worked around. Everything else goes into `docs/tracking/findings.md` with why it cannot be solved now.
